# Licensing Strategy Note

This file is a planning note, not legal advice or a completed license audit.

## Current decision

The selected authoritative lineage preserves the repository's existing MIT root license. Repository-history reconciliation does not change that license.

The GOSP Forge business plan recommends evaluating Apache-2.0 for public schemas, SDKs, conformance tests, and reference adapters because of its permissive terms and express patent grant. That recommendation requires deliberate legal/owner review before any license change or mixed-licensing design.

## Contribution rule

Do not copy source code, CAD, models, textures, datasets, standards text, or manufacturer assets merely because they are publicly accessible.

For every material third-party dependency or asset, record:

- source and version;
- governing license or terms;
- modifications;
- distribution mode;
- notices and source obligations;
- responsible maintainer or owner;
- security and support status when relevant.

Reciprocal GPL, AGPL, MPL, or LGPL dependencies require deliberate architecture and legal review rather than assumptions based on process separation.
