# Owner-Controlled Validation Gate

Date: 2026-08-14
Status: Governing Phase-0C operating model while outreach is prohibited

## Permanent communication boundary

Codex and project automation must not initiate or respond to email, direct messages, invitations, scheduling, interviews, sales activity, or other external communications for GOSP Forge. Historical outreach records remain provenance only.

## Evidence that may be processed

Gate-A evidence may be evaluated only when the owner independently supplies it to the local project, such as:

- an attributable feedback record already obtained by the owner;
- an owner-provided participant session record with consent and retention terms;
- a payment or pilot instrument supplied by the owner;
- unsolicited inbound evidence that the owner chooses to provide, without any reply by Codex.

No delivery receipt, automatic response, repository view, download, star, or unverified statement counts as organizational confirmation or willingness to pay.

## Gate status

Gate A remains unmet until evidence supports three distinct organizational confirmations and one paid-pilot commitment. The software and internal pilot package may be improved while the gate is unmet, but the project must not represent external validation, a design partnership, product-market fit, or a paid pilot.

## Pilot execution boundary

The repository may prepare a bounded pilot specification and local demo. Codex may execute only the internal technical portion. Participant selection, communication, consent, commercial terms, and externally attributable conclusions remain owner-controlled inputs and cannot be inferred or fabricated.
