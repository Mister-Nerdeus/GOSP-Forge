# Phase-0C Participant Selection and Outreach Plan

Status: Planning only; no organization has been contacted or associated with GOSP Forge

## Objective

Select an initial set of ten organizations for structured problem-discovery conversations about reproducible engineering evidence. The purpose is to test workflow value and rejection conditions, not to claim partnership, endorsement, validation, or sales progress.

## Portfolio mix

The first ten candidates should span:

| Participant group | Target count | Reason |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Engineering education or project-based learning programs | 3 | Frequent review, handoff, and explainability needs; lower-risk synthetic demonstrations are possible. |
| University or public-interest engineering laboratories | 2 | Potential bounded problems with explicit evidence and reproducibility requirements. |
| Modular construction, fabrication, or manufacturing organizations | 2 | Configuration, review, and evidence-reconstruction pain may be material. |
| Engineering consultancy or professional-review organization | 1 | Can test whether the evidence package reduces review effort without implying professional approval. |
| Water, energy, or government innovation program | 2 | Can test domain transfer and public-interest evidence requirements. |

## Selection score

Score each candidate from 0 to 2 on each dimension:

1. recognizable evidence/reproducibility problem;
2. access to a relevant participant role;
3. availability of a bounded, non-sensitive example;
4. ability to provide structured feedback;
5. plausible authority to sponsor a discovery or pilot;
6. fit with current local-only, synthetic-demo limitations;
7. manageable confidentiality and professional-boundary risk.

Prioritize total score, portfolio balance, and reachable participant roles. Do not infer interest from public mission statements.

## Required research record

For every proposed candidate, record:

- organization and participant group;
- official public source supporting the fit hypothesis;
- target role, not a guessed individual identity;
- hypothesized workflow problem;
- proposed bounded demo angle;
- confidentiality/professional-boundary risk;
- selection score and rationale;
- contact status, initially `not authorized / not contacted`.

## Outreach gate

Before any message is sent:

1. the owner approves the named candidate and target role;
2. the exact message is reviewed;
3. note-taking, attribution, privacy, and retention rules are ready;
4. the message states that GOSP Forge is an early local prototype and makes no professional, physical-validation, certification, or production-readiness claim;
5. no pilot, payment, NDA, publicity, or follow-up commitment is offered without separate authority.

## Operating sequence

1. Research and score at least fifteen possibilities from official public sources.
2. Select a balanced ten-candidate working list.
3. Owner-approve a first wave of three to five contacts.
4. Conduct interviews using the structured script and demo runbook.
5. Capture each interview as a separate attributable evidence record with the participant's privacy choices.
6. Re-score the remaining list using observed feedback.
7. Update Gate A only when evidence satisfies its definitions.
