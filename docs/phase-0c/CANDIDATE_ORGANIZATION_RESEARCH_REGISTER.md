Status: Research complete; no outreach authorized or performed

This register converts public mission and program information into hypotheses for discovery interviews. Inclusion does not indicate awareness, interest, partnership, endorsement, or validation by any organization. Target roles are organizational functions, not identified individuals. Every contact remains `not authorized / not contacted` until the repository owner approves the organization, role, and exact message.

Scores use the seven 0–2 dimensions in the [participant selection plan](PARTICIPANT_SELECTION_AND_OUTREACH_PLAN.md), for a maximum of 14. They are planning estimates, not externally verified facts.

## Portfolio check

The recommended ten provide three education-oriented settings (Purdue EPICS, Olin SCOPE, and MIT D-Lab), two public-interest engineering settings (Engineering for Change and Engineers Without Borders USA), two built-environment organizations (Georgia Tech DBL and NIBS BIM Council), one modular-construction association (Modular Building Institute), and two public technical-assistance settings (NYSP2I and NIST MEP). Some organizations span categories; the classification is solely for portfolio balance.

Before outreach, the owner should approve a first wave of three to five named organizations and target roles. A practical first wave is Georgia Tech DBL, Purdue EPICS, Engineering for Change, NYSP2I, and Olin SCOPE because they combine high fit with bounded synthetic-demo options. This recommendation is inferred from public program descriptions and has not been tested with the organizations.
