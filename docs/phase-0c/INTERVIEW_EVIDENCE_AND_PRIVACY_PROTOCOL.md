# Phase-0C Interview Evidence and Privacy Protocol

Status: Prospective operating control; no interview evidence has been collected

## Before collecting evidence

Ask the participant to choose:

- whether notes may be taken;
- whether their organization may be named or must be categorized only by type;
- whether direct quotations may be recorded;
- whether the record may be stored in the repository, in an access-controlled archive, or only as an anonymized summary;
- whether follow-up is welcome;
- whether any material is confidential, export-controlled, regulated, proprietary, or subject to professional-review restrictions.

Do not record personal contact details, confidential inputs, or unpublished problem data in a public repository.

## Evidence levels

| Level | Meaning | Gate A treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observation | A participant described a workflow or problem. | Interview count only. |
| Value confirmation | An attributable participant explicitly said the proposed workflow addresses a real organizational problem. | May count toward the three-organization threshold after review. |
| Design-partner confirmation | An authorized participant explicitly committed time or problem access for structured follow-up. | Track separately; not a paid commitment. |
| Paid-pilot commitment | An authorized participant explicitly agreed that the organization will pay for a defined discovery or pilot, subject to recorded conditions. | May count toward the paid threshold after owner review. |

Do not promote evidence to a higher level based on inference.

## Record lifecycle

1. Create one evidence record per interview using `EXTERNAL_FEEDBACK_EVIDENCE_TEMPLATE.json`.
2. Give the record a non-identifying ID until attribution permission is confirmed.
3. Separate public summaries from restricted notes and attachments.
4. Store only consented, minimum-necessary information.
5. Record contradictions, rejections, and uncertainty.
6. Review the record for secrets, private paths, personal data, and unsupported claims.
7. Update `GATE_A_TRACKER.md` only after the record passes evidence and consent review.
8. Correct or withdraw a record if the participant changes permission or the evidence is found inaccurate.

## Prohibited interpretations

- Interview participation is not endorsement.
- Positive feedback is not professional approval or external reproduction.
- A design-partner conversation is not a contract.
- A tentative budget discussion is not a paid-pilot commitment.
- A real problem description does not authorize use of confidential data.
