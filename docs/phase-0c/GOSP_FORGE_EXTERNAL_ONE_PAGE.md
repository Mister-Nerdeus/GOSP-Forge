# GOSP Forge — External One-Page Explanation

## The problem

Engineering work often separates requirements, assumptions, models, calculations, test evidence, decisions, and review history across tools and handoffs. That makes results hard to explain, reproduce, challenge, and reuse.

## What the minimal loop demonstrates

GOSP Forge structures a bounded problem as a versioned Challenge, accepts a versioned Submission, runs a recorded Evaluation, links Claims to Evidence, and compares controlled candidates while showing what changed and what stayed fixed. The demonstrator preserves material identities, execution evidence, replay information, math, model fidelity, assumptions, limitations, and unresolved proof obligations.

## What GOSP does not claim

The current demonstrator does not provide professional engineering approval, physical validation, certification, regulatory approval, production deployment readiness, independent external reproduction, durable multi-user storage, or product-market validation. It coordinates evidence; it does not replace engineers, laboratories, regulators, teachers, or established engineering tools.

## Why reproducibility and evidence matter

Reproducible inputs and explicit evidence make it easier to locate disagreements, distinguish execution differences from engineering differences, review a result, preserve negative findings, and reuse work without overstating confidence.

## Intended participant and customer groups

- engineering educators and university faculty;
- modular construction and prefab manufacturers;
- engineering consultancies and university laboratories;
- water, energy, manufacturing, and process organizations;
- government innovation/program offices;
- professional reviewers and test organizations.

External feedback is evidence. Negative feedback, rejection criteria, privacy needs, and unwillingness to pay must be recorded rather than normalized away.
